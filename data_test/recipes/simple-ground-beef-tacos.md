# Simple Ground Beef Tacos

## Ingredients
- 1 lb lean ground beef
- 1 packet mild taco seasoning
- 1/2 cup water
- 6 small taco shells or flour tortillas
- Shredded cheddar cheese for topping

## Instructions
1. In a skillet over medium-high heat, cook and brown the ground beef until no longer pink. Drain excess fat.
2. Stir in the taco seasoning packet and 1/2 cup of water.
3. Simmer for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce has thickened.
4. Spoon the beef into taco shells and top with shredded cheese.
