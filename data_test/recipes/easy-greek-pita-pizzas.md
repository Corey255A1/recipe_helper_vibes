# Easy Greek Pita Pizzas

## Ingredients
- 3 pita breads
- 1/2 cup hummus
- 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
- 1/2 cup crumbled feta cheese
- 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes, halved

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C) and place pitas on a baking sheet.
2. Spread a layer of hummus evenly over each pita bread.
3. Top with shredded mozzarella, crumbled feta, and cherry tomato halves.
4. Bake for 8-10 minutes until the edges of the pita are crispy and the cheese is melted.
5. Slice into small quarters and serve.

## Notes
A fun, customized pizza night that fits the Mediterranean theme.
