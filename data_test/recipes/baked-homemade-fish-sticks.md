# Crispy Baked Homemade Fish Sticks

## Ingredients
- 1 lb white fish fillets (like cod), cut into finger-sized strips
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- 2 eggs, lightly beaten
- 1 cup Panko breadcrumbs
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- Salt to taste

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Set up a breading station with three shallow bowls: flour in the first, beaten eggs in the second, and Panko mixed with garlic powder and salt in the third.
3. Dredge each fish strip in flour, dip in egg, then press into the Panko until well coated.
4. Arrange fish sticks on the prepared baking sheet and bake for 12-15 minutes until golden and crispy.
