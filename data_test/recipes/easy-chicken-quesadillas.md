# Easy 3-Ingredient Chicken Quesadillas

## Ingredients
- 2 cups cooked chicken, shredded
- 1.5 cups shredded Mexican-blend cheese
- 6 small flour tortillas

## Instructions
1. Place three tortillas on a clean surface or griddle.
2. Divide the shredded chicken and cheese evenly among the tortillas, spreading on one half of each.
3. Fold the tortillas in half to cover the filling.
4. Cook in a dry skillet over medium heat for 3-4 minutes per side until the cheese is melted and the tortillas are golden brown.
5. Cut into small triangles before serving to make it easy for your toddler.

## Notes
Extremely quick and highly toddler-approved.
