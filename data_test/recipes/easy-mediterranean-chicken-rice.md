# Easy Mediterranean Chicken and Rice Skillet

## Ingredients
- 1 lb chicken breasts, diced into bite-sized pieces
- 1 cup long-grain white rice
- 2 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1/2 cup canned sliced black olives

## Instructions
1. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat and brown the diced chicken for 5 minutes.
2. Stir in the white rice, chicken broth, dried oregano, and black olives.
3. Bring the mixture to a boil, then cover and reduce heat to low.
4. Simmer for 20 minutes until the rice is tender and the liquid is absorbed.
5. Fluff with a fork and let cool slightly before serving.
