# Sheet Pan Shrimp Fajitas

## Ingredients
- 1 lb peeled and deveined shrimp
- 1 bell pepper, sliced
- 1 small onion, sliced
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 tablespoon mild taco seasoning
- 6 small flour tortillas for serving

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. On a large baking sheet, toss shrimp, bell pepper, and onion with olive oil and taco seasoning.
3. Spread the ingredients in a single even layer.
4. Bake for 10-12 minutes until the shrimp are pink and cooked through.
5. Serve inside warmed tortillas.
