# Easy Lemon Butter Baked Salmon

## Ingredients
- 3 salmon fillets (approx. 4-6 oz each)
- 2 tablespoons melted butter
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C) and line a baking sheet with foil.
2. Place salmon fillets skin-side down on the prepared sheet.
3. Whisk together melted butter, lemon juice, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
4. Brush the butter mixture evenly over the salmon fillets.
5. Bake for 12-15 minutes until the salmon flakes easily with a fork.

## Notes
Flakes easily for toddlers. Serve with a simple side of steamed peas.
