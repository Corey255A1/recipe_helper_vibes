# Greek Lemon Chicken and Potatoes

## Ingredients
- 2 lbs chicken thighs, bone-in and skin-on
- 4 large potatoes, cut into wedges
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 1/4 cup lemon juice
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, oregano, salt, and pepper.
3. Add chicken thighs and potato wedges, tossing to coat thoroughly.
4. Transfer everything to a large baking dish, arranging chicken skin-side up.
5. Bake for 45-50 minutes until chicken is golden and cooked through and potatoes are tender.
