# Sheet Pan Garlic Butter Tilapia and Green Beans

## Ingredients
- 3 tilapia fillets
- 12 oz fresh green beans, trimmed
- 2 tablespoons melted butter
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 1 tablespoon olive oil

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C) and grease a large sheet pan.
2. Toss green beans with olive oil, salt, and pepper, then spread them onto one side of the pan.
3. Place tilapia fillets on the other side of the pan.
4. Stir together melted butter, garlic powder, and lemon juice. Drizzle over both the fish and green beans.
5. Bake for 12-15 minutes until the fish flakes easily and green beans are tender-crisp.

## Notes
Tilapia is mild and soft, making it great for young children.
