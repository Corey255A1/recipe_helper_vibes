# Easy Baked Beef Meatballs

## Ingredients
- 1 lb ground beef
- 1/2 cup fine breadcrumbs
- 1 egg
- 1/4 cup grated parmesan cheese
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. In a large bowl, mix together the ground beef, breadcrumbs, egg, parmesan cheese, garlic powder, and salt until just combined.
3. Roll the mixture into 1-inch meatballs and arrange them on the baking sheet.
4. Bake for 18-20 minutes until cooked through and lightly browned.

## Notes
Great for making on Sunday to eat on Monday. Soft enough for a 2-year-old.
